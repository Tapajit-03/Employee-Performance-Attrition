EMPLOYEE ATTRITION ANALYSIS - BUSINESS INSIGHTS
=======================================================

Total employees analyzed: 1470
Employees who left: 237
Overall attrition rate: 16.12%


1. OVERTIME RISK
Employees working overtime had an attrition rate of 30.53%.
Employees without overtime had an attrition rate of 10.44%.
Recommendation: HR should monitor overtime workloads and investigate whether excessive work hours are contributing to employee turnover.


2. DEPARTMENT RISK
The Sales department had the highest observed attrition rate at 20.63%.
Recommendation: Management should examine workload, compensation, career progression, and employee satisfaction within higher-risk departments.


3. JOB SATISFACTION
Employees with job satisfaction level 1 had an attrition rate of 22.84%.
Recommendation: HR could conduct employee feedback surveys and identify workplace factors associated with low job satisfaction.


4. MACHINE LEARNING FINDINGS
The logistic regression model identified overtime, business travel, job role, marital status, job level, and career progression variables among the strongest model-associated features.
These factors should be treated as indicators for further investigation rather than direct causes of employee attrition.


5. MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
• Monitor overtime and workload patterns.
• Focus retention analysis on higher-risk departments.
• Investigate low job satisfaction through employee feedback.
• Review career progression and promotion opportunities.
• Use the ML model as a decision-support tool rather than as the sole basis for HR decisions.
